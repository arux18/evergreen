In use

Same data, drawn with the first six and the first five.

No greens. The closest thing is the Teal family, which starts at hue 186. Your gain colour --pos #148338 is at hue 150 — far enough that nothing here reads as a positive value.

No gold either. Ember stops at apricot. A deep amber is the one colour sRGB cannot darken without it going muddy, so the family runs pale pink → salmon → peach → apricot → warm orange and never reaches yellow.

Every fill has a deeper twin. --catN-deep is the companion for anything thin — line strokes, dots, legend squares — because a pastel disappears at 2px. And a label sitting on a fill is always dark ink, never white.
